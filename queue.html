Reading a rate before you accept

$12/hr search rating with an unlimited clock and $40/hr red-teaming with a 45-second timer are not the same job. Effective rate = (rubric score ÷ time actually spent) × your rate, adjusted for the rework loop every sub-70 item puts you through. The earnings page computes this per task so you can see which task types are quietly costing you money.
