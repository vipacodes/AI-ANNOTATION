How to use this page

1 · match yourself

Pick by the gate, not the rate. A $120/hr credential-gated queue is worthless to you if you can't clear the credential step. Pick the highest-paying gate you can actually pass today.

2 · rehearse that shape

Every practice set is the task format of that platform, not its content. Ranking sets, golden answers, rubric grading, code review, guideline rating — the mechanics transfer, the answers do not.

3 · then go be boring

Apply to two of these, not five. Pass one assessment properly, keep the other as your fallback queue. Then log your hours in the ledger so you know your real rate by week three.
