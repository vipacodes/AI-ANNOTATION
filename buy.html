What a key is, mechanically

A key is a signed string, not an account: id.signature.expiry. You paste it once, it sets a cookie, the server verifies the signature against its secret and serves the protected pages. Revoke any key at any time with one command. There is no password to leak, no database of you, and no email list.

Your practice history stays in your own browser's storage. I never see your scores.

Read this before you pay

This does not get you hired, and no score here counts for anything outside this site. It will not tell you the real questions — those are non-disclosure-protected and change per project.
