The assessment they give you is the job you don't get paid for.

Most AI-training marketplaces screen you with 2–5 hours of unpaid tasks — response ranking, rubric writing, reasoning-trace review, an AI-use detector — then reject roughly 4 in 5 applicants with a template email. Here is that assessment, with the grading rules shown open, so the first one you take for real is not the one you learn on.

What is real here, and what isn't

The task formats, rubrics and failure modes are modelled on how these queues actually score you — pairwise agreement instead of a single right answer, precision weighted against recall, "first erroneous step" traps, and hidden integrity probes. The client, brief, dollar rates and queue names are fictional, deliberately: this is not an Outlier clone, not a Handshake AI clone, not a vendor portal, and nothing here is a credential or proof of vetting.

If anyone shows you a site like this and asks for money — for "activation", "ID verification", a "US location", or a training course — that's the scam. Real platforms never charge you. Full red-flag list →
